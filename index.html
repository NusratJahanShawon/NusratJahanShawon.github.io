Automation lab

Workflows that run while the team sleeps.

Each of these runs in production at Provision Capital, taking repetitive work off the team.

AI venture assessor

Researches and scores every new venture submission, then posts the verdict to Slack and Google Sheets.

  1. New submission in Sheets
  2. Research, assess, double-check
  3. Verdict to Sheets and Slack

Leads to GoHighLevel

New leads from websites, newsletters and the CRM land in GoHighLevel with the right tag, so each one starts the right follow-up sequence.

  1. New lead comes in
  2. Tagged by type
  3. Follow-up starts in GoHighLevel

Vendor onboarding

A vendor fills in one form and lands straight on their contract, ready to e-sign. Every signup is logged in Google Sheets.

  1. Vendor fills the form
  2. Logged in Sheets
  3. Contract ready to sign

Lawn service confirmations

Twice a month it turns the lawn schedule into PDFs, asks for a yes in Slack, then drafts a bilingual email to the vendor.

  1. On the 1st and 15th
  2. Approved in Slack
  3. Email draft with PDFs

AI photo editor in Slack

Mention the bot with a photo and say what to change. It replies with the edited image, and you can keep editing in the thread.

  1. Photo and instruction
  2. Edited with AI
  3. New image in the thread

Property tour video bot

Places an agent into a property photo and turns the photos into a short video tour, right inside Slack.

  1. Ask in the thread
  2. Agent placed, video made
  3. Video posted to Slack

Slack thread to Google Drive

Mention the bot in a thread and every photo and video in it is saved to a new Drive folder, with the link posted back.

  1. Bot mentioned in a thread
  2. Files collected
  3. Shareable Drive folder
